For iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Iron, made simple.

Ferron guides you through kettlebell programs and workouts, times your intervals, and tracks your runs and skipping sessions. Wear your Apple Watch and every session lands in Apple Health with your heart rate and calories. There's no account to create, no ads and no tracking.

  • Programs for every level. Follow a plan from your first swing to advanced complexes, or build your own workouts and programs.
  • Guided workouts. Exercises come with looping videos, and Ferron keeps track of your rounds, rests and weights as you go.
  • Interval timers. EMOM, AMRAP, Tabata, For Time, sets and a stopwatch, with voice cues that keep going when the screen is locked.
  • Runs and skipping rope. Outdoor runs with a map of your route, treadmill runs with Bluetooth treadmills, and skipping sessions that your Apple Watch counts for you.
  • Apple Watch and Apple Health. Live heart rate and calories on your wrist, and your workouts saved to Health and your Activity rings. Bluetooth heart rate straps work too.
  • See your progress. Streaks, weekly goals, trophies for finished programs, and widgets on your Home Screen.
