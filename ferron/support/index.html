Training

Which program should I start with?

Ferron suggests programs that match the fitness level in your profile, and they're grouped from beginner to advanced. If you're new to kettlebells, start with Introduction to Kettlebell. You can change your level at any time in Settings › Profile.

How do I set my kettlebell weight?

Set your usual bell in Settings › Profile › Working Kettlebell. Workouts start from that weight, and you can change the weight for any exercise during a workout. Kilograms or pounds is under Settings › Profile › Weight.

Can I make my own workouts and programs?

Yes. Build a workout from the exercise library and save it, or put several workouts together into your own program.

What do EMOM, AMRAP and Tabata mean?

Settings › Workout Guide explains every workout and timer type with examples.

The timer stops when I lock my phone

Make sure Settings › Background Timer is on. Ferron then keeps timing and playing its cues with the screen locked, and shows the timer on your Lock Screen. Screen On keeps the display awake during a session instead.

Runs and skipping

My outdoor run has no route

Ferron needs location access while you use it. Check iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Ferron and choose While Using the App with Precise Location on. You can lock your phone during the run: tracking carries on, and iOS shows a blue location indicator while it does.

Connecting a treadmill

Ferron works with Bluetooth treadmills that support the Fitness Machine standard (FTMS), which most treadmills made in the last few years do. Turn the treadmill on, start a treadmill run, and choose it from the list. Treadmills often read a little long or short, so Settings › Treadmill Calibration learns the difference from your runs and corrects the distance.

Using a heart rate strap

Pair a Bluetooth chest strap or armband in Settings › Heart Rate Monitor. Ferron then connects to it by itself when a workout or run starts and shows your heart rate live. If you also wear an Apple Watch, the watch's heart rate is used.

How are skipping jumps counted?

With your Apple Watch on, Ferron counts your jumps from the watch's motion sensors as you skip. Without a watch, or if the count is off, you can type in the right number on the summary screen before you save.

Apple Watch and Apple Health

Setting up the Apple Watch app

Install Ferron on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. Then start a workout, run or skipping session on your iPhone as usual, and Ferron opens on your watch by itself, showing your heart rate, calories and the current exercise.

My watch didn't start, or stopped showing my heart rate

Keep the watch on your wrist and unlocked, with your iPhone nearby and Bluetooth on. Ferron tries again by itself if the watch doesn't respond at first. Your iPhone keeps recording the workout either way, so your session is never lost, and if the watch saved it to Health, Ferron picks up its heart rate and calories afterwards.

Connecting Apple Health

Turn it on in Settings › Apple Health. If you said no earlier, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps › Ferron, and turn the switches on.

A workout isn't in Apple Health

Workouts are saved to Health by your Apple Watch, so a session recorded without the watch has no Health entry. If you wore the watch, give it a moment to sync with your iPhone. Then open the workout in Ferron and tap the grey Health icon at the top: if Health has the workout, Ferron links it and fills in your heart rate and calories.

Subscription

What's included?

Ferron is a subscription, monthly or yearly, and both start with a free week if you haven't had a Ferron trial before. It unlocks everything: all programs and workouts, timers, runs and skipping, Apple Watch and Apple Health, Bluetooth treadmills and heart rate straps, and iCloud backup. Without a subscription you can still open the app, look at your history and use Settings.

How do I cancel?

Go to Settings › Membership › Manage Subscription in Ferron, or to your Apple Account's subscriptions in iPhone Settings. Cancel at least a day before the trial or the current period ends, and you won't be charged again. You keep access until the end of the time you've paid for.

My subscription doesn't show on a new device

Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen while you're signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Backing up and moving to a new iPhone

In Settings › Profile › Backup you can export a backup file and import it on another device. Subscribers can also back up to iCloud, automatically when they leave the app or whenever they like, and restore it on a new iPhone. Restoring adds the backup to what's already on the device and never deletes anything.

Why are heart rate and calories missing after an iCloud restore?

To protect your health data, Ferron leaves heart rate, calories and body weight out of the iCloud backup. After a restore, Ferron reads heart rate and calories back from Apple Health for workouts your watch recorded. A backup file you export yourself keeps everything.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Profile › Clear All History deletes every workout, run, skipping and timer session, removes the matching workouts from Apple Health, and clears them from your iCloud backup. Delete iCloud Backup in the Backup screen removes the backup itself. Deleting the app removes everything else from your device.
