Listening

A station won't play or keeps stopping

Every station runs its own stream, so sometimes a station goes offline for a while or moves to a new address. Try another station first to make sure your connection is fine. If only that one station fails, the problem is usually on the station's side, and it often comes back by itself. On a weak connection, it can help to lower Settings › Preferences › Audio Quality.

I can't find a station

Radion gets its stations from Radio Browser, a free directory that listeners keep up to date together. If a station is missing, you can add it there so everyone gets it. If you know the station's stream address, you can also add it yourself in Radion as a Custom Station.

Why do I see stations from my country?

Top Nearby looks at your device's region (Settings › General › Language & Region). It never uses your location. To see the top stations in another country, change the region, or simply browse that country in Browse.

Can I set a sleep timer?

Yes. Open the player and choose the sleep timer. The music stops when the time runs out.

Songs

Song ID says "No match"

Song ID listens to the station's stream for a few seconds, so it works best while music is playing, not during talk, ads or jingles. Give it another try a bit later in the song. It doesn't use your microphone, so it doesn't matter how noisy the room is.

Why can't I like every song?

You can like a song once Radion has matched what the station is playing to a real song in the catalogue. Some stations don't send song details at all, or only send the name of the show. Song ID still works on those stations.

How does the Apple Music playlist work?

Turn on Settings › Library › Apple Music Playlist, and Radion will add every song you identify or like to a playlist called "Radion" in your Apple Music library. Removing a song in Radion doesn't remove it from the playlist. To do that, edit the playlist in the Music app.

Premium

What does Premium include?

Listening is free. Premium unlocks Favorites, Custom Stations, Song ID, Recording, Time Machine and the Sleep Timer. You can try all of it free for 3 days. After that, you pay once and it's yours to keep. There's no subscription.

I bought Premium but it's locked on a new device

Go to Settings › Premium › Restore Purchases while you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it. Premium works on every device that uses the same Apple Account.

My trial ended. What now?

The trial belongs to your Apple Account, so you only get it once, even if you reinstall the app. When it ends, you can buy Premium or take up the offer the app shows you.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all purchases, so refunds go through Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your stations and data

How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Turn on Settings › Data › Backup & Restore › iCloud Sync on each device, and make sure they're all signed in to the same Apple Account. Your favorites, custom stations, history, songs and recordings will then stay the same everywhere.

How do I back up or move my data?

In Settings › Data › Backup & Restore you can export a backup file, with or without your recordings. Import it on another device, and choose whether to add it to what's already there or replace it.

Can I share or export recordings?

No. Recordings are for listening to later in Radion, and they sync to your other devices through iCloud. The audio belongs to the station that broadcast it, so Radion doesn't let you share it.

Apple Watch and CarPlay

Setting up the Apple Watch app

Install Radion on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. Open Radion on your iPhone, and your favorites, custom stations and saved songs will show up on the watch. The button at the top of the watch's home screen lets you choose where the radio plays: on the watch itself, or on your iPhone with the watch as a remote.

The watch says it can't reach my iPhone

To use the watch as a remote, your iPhone needs to be nearby with Bluetooth or Wi-Fi turned on. Open Radion on the iPhone once, then try again. Playing on the watch itself works without your phone, over Wi-Fi or cellular.

Using Radion in the car

Connect your iPhone to CarPlay and open Radion on the car's screen. You'll find your Favorites, Recently Played and Browse there.
