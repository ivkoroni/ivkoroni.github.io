Listening

A station won't play or keeps stopping

Each station runs its own stream, so a station can be offline for a while or move to a new address. Try another station to rule out your connection. If only one station fails, it's usually that station's stream, and it often comes back on its own. Setting Settings › Preferences › Audio Quality to a lower quality can help on a weak connection.

I can't find a station

Radion's stations come from Radio Browser, a community-run directory. If a station is missing, you can add it there for everyone. If you have its stream address, you can also add it yourself as a Custom Station.

Why do I see stations from my country?

Top Nearby uses your device's region (Settings › General › Language & Region), never your location. Change the region to see another country's top stations, or browse any country in Browse.

Can I set a sleep timer?

Yes. Open the player and choose the sleep timer, and playback stops when it runs out.

Songs

Song ID says "No match"

Song ID listens to the station's own stream for a few seconds, so it works best while music is playing, not during talk, ads or jingles. Try again a little later in the song. It doesn't use your microphone, so the room can be as noisy as you like.

Why can't I like every song?

You can like a song once Radion has matched what the station is broadcasting to a song in the catalogue. Some stations don't send song details, or send only the show name. Song ID still works for those.

How does the Apple Music playlist work?

Turn on Settings › Library › Apple Music Playlist and Radion adds every song you identify or like to a "Radion" playlist in your Apple Music library. Removing a song in Radion doesn't remove it from the playlist; edit the playlist in the Music app.

Premium

What does Premium include?

Listening is free. Premium unlocks Favorites, Custom Stations, Song ID, Recording, Time Machine and the Sleep Timer. You can try it all free for 3 days, then keep it with a single one-time purchase. There's no subscription.

I bought Premium but it's locked on a new device

Go to Settings › Premium › Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Premium works on all your devices with the same Apple Account.

My trial ended. What now?

The trial is tied to your Apple Account, so it runs once, even after reinstalling. When it ends you can buy Premium, or follow the offer the app shows you.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your stations and data

How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Turn on Settings › Data › Backup & Restore › iCloud Sync on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account. Favorites, custom stations, history, songs and recordings then stay in step.

How do I back up or move my data?

In Settings › Data › Backup & Restore you can export a backup file, with or without recordings, and import it on another device, merging it with what's there or replacing it.

Can I share or export recordings?

No. Recordings are for listening to later inside Radion, and they sync to your other devices through iCloud. The audio belongs to the broadcaster, so Radion doesn't let you share it.

Apple Watch and CarPlay

Setting up the Apple Watch app

Install Radion on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. Your favorites, custom stations and saved songs appear on the watch after you open Radion on your iPhone. Use the button at the top of the watch's home screen to choose whether stations play on the watch itself or on your iPhone, with the watch as a remote.

The watch says it can't reach my iPhone

Remote control needs your iPhone nearby with Bluetooth or Wi-Fi on. Open Radion on the iPhone once, then try again. Playing on the watch itself works without the phone, over Wi-Fi or cellular.

Using Radion in the car

Connect your iPhone to CarPlay and open Radion on the car's screen, where you'll find your Favorites, Recently Played and Browse.
