Privacy Policy

Your listening is your business.

Radion has no account, no ads and no tracking. The developer doesn't collect, receive or sell any of your data.

Effective 14 October 2026

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no ad or tracking libraries.
  • No servers of our own. Radion doesn't send anything to the developer. What you save stays on your device and in your own iCloud.
  • No location. Local stations come from your device's region setting, not GPS.
  • No microphone. Song ID listens to the station's stream, not to the room you're in.

This policy explains what that means in practice: what stays on your device, what goes to your iCloud, and which outside services the app talks to so it can play radio. It covers the Radion app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, along with its widgets and CarPlay.

Who is responsible

Radion is made by Ardit Koroni, an independent developer in Sweden, who is the data controller for this policy. You can reach me at arditkoroni@gmail.com.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Radion has no backend and never sends your activity, preferences, saved songs, recordings or device details to the developer. I can't see which stations you play, what you search for, or what you save.

The only time I receive anything about you is if you email me. I then get your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to reply. See Contacting me.

What stays on your device

Radion keeps the following on your device so the app works the way you left it:

  • favorite stations, custom stations you've added, and recently played stations
  • Recognized & Liked Songs
  • recordings you make of a stream
  • settings such as language, style, theme, equalizer, audio quality, news ticker source and sleep timer
  • a cache of station logos, song artwork and station lists, so the app is faster and uses less data
  • whether Premium, the 3-day trial or the free unlock is active on your Apple Account

The widgets, the Apple Watch app and CarPlay read the same data. The watch gets your stations, saved songs and what's playing directly from your iPhone, over Apple's connection between your own devices.

You can delete this data at any time. Clear recently played or the cache in Settings › Data, delete songs and recordings in their lists, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device.

iCloud sync and backups

With iCloud Sync turned on (Settings › Data › Backup & Restore), Radion keeps your favorites, custom stations, history, recognized and liked songs, and recordings in sync across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is stored in your iCloud, in space that belongs to Radion, and Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer can't access it. You can turn sync off at any time, and you can manage or delete Radion's iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.

A backup you export is a file you create and keep yourself. Radion doesn't upload it anywhere.

Services the app connects to

To play radio, Radion talks directly from your device to the services below. Like any website you visit, each one sees your device's IP address and the request it receives, and handles that under its own privacy policy. Radion sends none of them an identifier for you, and it doesn't combine anything across services.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

So that "Play … in Radion" and Siri Suggestions work, Radion gives the system the names of your stations and tells it, on your device, when you play one. This is handled by Apple's on-device intelligence under Apple's policies, and you can turn it off for Radion in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Widgets show your stations and what's playing, using data that stays on your device.

Permissions

  • Apple Music: asked for only when you turn on Apple Music Playlist. You can revoke it in your device's Settings.
  • Location: never requested.
  • Microphone: never requested.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): never requested, because Radion doesn't track you.

Children

Radion is a general-audience app, not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone, children included. Live radio is chosen and broadcast by third-party stations, so parents may want to use Screen Time to manage what younger listeners can access.

Your rights

If you're in the EU, the EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Because Radion doesn't send your data to the developer, almost everything about you is already in your own hands, on your device and in your iCloud, and you can view or delete it there.

For anything you've sent me by email, write to me and I'll answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY); elsewhere, it is the authority where you live. Residents of California and other places with similar laws have equivalent rights. Radion doesn't sell or share personal information.

Contacting me

If you email me, I use your address and message only to answer you, on the basis of my legitimate interest in replying to people who write to me. Email goes through my email provider (Google). I keep the conversation only as long as it's useful for helping you, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

Changes to this policy

If Radion changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
